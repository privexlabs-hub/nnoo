What Nnọọ is

Two hands, one body.

A place where an elder's voice can travel across generations — where a child in London hears the same word their grandmother learned in Nnewi.

Our promises

Trust is not a page in Settings. It is the product.

01Named

Every elder is credited with their town and dialect.

02Paid

When a recording passes Ikpe, the elder is paid.

03Chosen

The elder decides who may hear — and can change it.

04Never sold

Recordings and personal data are not sold, and elder voices are not used to train outside AI without consent.

05Heard

When an elder says no, the system listens.
